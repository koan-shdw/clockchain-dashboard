Smart Contract Scheduling

When you schedule a contract we quote a cost range and charge the mid-range estimate. After execution you're credited or debited the difference against your CCTT balance. Canceled contracts refund minus a 15% fee.

Worried about overage? Set up auto-pay to top up CCTT automatically. Unpaid overage can put future schedulings on hold.
